Gustav Klimt (1862-1918)
Portrait of Adele Bloch-Bauer I, 1907
Oil, silver and gold on canvas, 138 x 138 cm

Provenance:
Ferdinand Bloch-Bauer, Vienna, commissioned from the artist, 1907.
Confiscated by the German authorities, Vienna, 1938.
Osterreichische Galerie Belvedere, Vienna, 1941.
Restituted to Maria Altmann and the heirs of Ferdinand Bloch-Bauer, Los Angeles, 2006.
Neue Galerie, New York, acquired from the above, 2006.
